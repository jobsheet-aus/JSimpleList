HELP & INFORMATION

Support

GoListGo works without an account for lists stored on your Android device. Signing in is optional and allows you to take selected lists online for synchronisation and sharing.

Using GoListGo

Start by creating a to-do, shopping or discussion list. Add items, tick them off and use Uncheck all to reuse a list. To share a list, open its sharing options and follow the sign-in steps if needed.

Browser access

Online lists can also be accessed through the GoListGo browser app. Lists stored only on your phone are not uploaded or available in a browser.

Privacy and account deletion

Read the privacy policy, terms or account deletion instructions.

Need assistance?

For technical issues, you can report a problem on GitHub. A dedicated email support address will be added when confirmed.
